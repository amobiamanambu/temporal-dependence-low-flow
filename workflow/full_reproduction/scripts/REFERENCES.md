## Time irreversibility and fluctuation concepts

- Crooks, G. E. (1999). Entropy production fluctuation theorem and the nonequilibrium work relation for free energy differences. *Physical Review E*, 60, 2721–2726. [doi:10.1103/PhysRevE.60.2721](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.60.2721).
- Seifert, U. (2005). Entropy production along a stochastic trajectory and an integral fluctuation theorem. *Physical Review Letters*, 95, 040602. [doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.95.040602](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.95.040602).
- Vavoulogiannis, S., et al. (2021). Multiscale temporal irreversibility of streamflow. *Hydrology*, 8, 63. [doi:10.3390/hydrology8020063](https://doi.org/10.3390/hydrology8020063).
- Mathai, J., & Mujumdar, P. P. (2022). Use of streamflow indices to identify the catchment drivers of hydrographs. *Hydrology and Earth System Sciences*, 26, 2019–2036. [doi:10.5194/hess-26-2019-2022](https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-26-2019-2022).

The last two papers mean that a manuscript must not claim the first application of time irreversibility to streamflow.

## Recession dynamics

- Brutsaert, W., & Nieber, J. L. (1977). Regionalized drought flow hydrographs from a mature glaciated plateau. *Water Resources Research*, 13, 637–643. [doi:10.1029/WR013i003p00637](https://doi.org/10.1029/WR013i003p00637).
- Rupp, D. E., & Selker, J. S. (2006). Information, artifacts, and noise in dQ/dt–Q recession analysis. *Advances in Water Resources*, 29, 154–160. [doi:10.1016/j.advwatres.2005.03.019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.advwatres.2005.03.019).
- Kirchner, J. W. (2009). Catchments as simple dynamical systems: Catchment characterization, rainfall–runoff modeling, and doing hydrology backward. *Water Resources Research*, 45, W02429. [doi:10.1029/2008WR006912](https://doi.org/10.1029/2008WR006912).
- Botter, G. (2010). Stochastic recession rates and the probabilistic structure of stream flows. *Water Resources Research*, 46, W12527. [doi:10.1029/2010WR009217](https://doi.org/10.1029/2010WR009217).
- Suweis, S., Bertuzzo, E., Botter, G., Porporato, A., Rodriguez-Iturbe, I., & Rinaldo, A. (2010). Impact of stochastic fluctuations in storage–discharge relations on streamflow distributions. *Water Resources Research*, 46, W03517. [doi:10.1029/2009WR008038](https://doi.org/10.1029/2009WR008038).
- Tashie, A., Pavelsky, T. M., & Band, L. E. (2020). An empirical reevaluation of streamflow recession analysis at the continental scale. *Water Resources Research*, 56. [doi:10.1029/2019WR025448](https://doi.org/10.1029/2019WR025448).
- McMahon, S., et al. (2026). Natural variability in master recession curves. *Hydrology and Earth System Sciences*, 30, 893–916. [doi:10.5194/hess-30-893-2026](https://doi.org/10.5194/hess-30-893-2026).

Suweis et al. supply a prior theoretical multiplicative-noise expectation. The present pipeline therefore tests that expectation empirically across catchments; it does not claim to invent stochastic storage–discharge variability.
